## 📱 How to Use

1. **Browse Restaurants**
   - Scroll through the beautiful restaurant grid
   - Each card shows name, location, phone, and ratings
   - Click any card to view the menu

2. **Order Food**
   - Click on a restaurant
   - Browse the menu items
   - Use +/- buttons to adjust quantities
   - Cart appears on the right side
   - Click "Place Order" to checkout

3. **Track Orders**
   - Click "My Orders" in navigation
   - See all your orders with status
   - Watch status updates in real-time
